## Overview
**Avatar: The Way of Water** is the 2022 sequel to *Avatar*, directed by **James Cameron**. It follows Jake Sully and his family as they leave their forest home and seek refuge with the *Metkayina* reef clan.
## Main Characters
- Jake Sully
- Neytiri
- Lo'ak
- Kiri
- Tonowari
- Ronal
